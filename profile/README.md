## RealM

AI revenue manager for independent hotels. Live.

RealM monitors the property, investigates demand shifts, recommends room rates, and carries out authorized actions through the PMS.

- Reads reservations and availability from the PMS continuously
- Forecasts demand from the property's own booking history
- Prices against the inventory that is actually left to sell
- Acts only on what a revenue manager has approved
- Shows no number it cannot trace to a dated source observation
