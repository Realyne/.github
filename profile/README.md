# 🚀 Realyne

Realyne is the revenue management layer for independent hotels.

Our platform, **RealM**, connects to a hotel's PMS, reconstructs what is really on the books, forecasts demand from the property's own booking history, and recommends rates — without ever writing back to the PMS on its own.

## ✨ Core Features

- 📊 **Live PMS Integration** — reservations and availability read continuously from the property management system, reconciled against the hotel's own figures
- 📈 **Booking-Curve Forecasting** — a Bayesian model that learns from reservations alone, updates every night, and needs no retraining
- 💶 **Contribution-Based Pricing** — rate recommendations optimised against real remaining inventory, held for a revenue manager to approve
- 🔍 **Traceable by Design** — every figure derives from a dated source observation, and the platform shows nothing it cannot support

## 🎯 Why Realyne

Revenue management software was built for chains, and priced for them. Independent hotels are left with a spreadsheet and an instinct, while the data needed to price well already sits in their PMS.

RealM turns that data into decisions a hotelier can check. It stays read-only to the PMS by default, explains where each number came from, and refuses to answer when it has no evidence — because a system that always produces a number is one nobody can trust.

## 🌐 Vision

To become the revenue infrastructure independent hotels actually run on — where every rate has a reason, every number has a source, and the hotelier stays in control.
